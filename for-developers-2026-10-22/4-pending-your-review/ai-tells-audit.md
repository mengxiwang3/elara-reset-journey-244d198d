# What makes Explorar and Círculos look AI-made (audit, 22 Oct 2026)

The owner looked at the unified prototypes and said: "try and make them somehow less claude like". This is the audit made before any change. It covers the shared system v1 as it stood:
- Explorar: `explore-refined-2026-10-21/v3-before-authored/artifact/`
- Círculos: `circles-review-2026-10-22/v3-before-authored/artifact/`

**How it was made**
- Both were rendered at 390 × 844 in headless Chromium.
  - Explorar: the overview, Talleres, Guías, the workshop detail, the audio detail and search («noche»).
  - Círculos: Mis círculos, Descubrir (also on the first visit), a circle feed (Trabajo y propósito), a post and the composer.
- The full captures are in `scratchpad/auth/before/`. The crops below are in `_shared/captures/tells/` (390 px wide, 1×).
- Each element was checked against three references:
  - **brand guidelines:** `Elara-Brand-Guidelines.pdf` (Oct 2026: palette, type, paper language, icons, do and don't), plus `deliverables/brand/identity-directions-2026-10-14/` and `deliverables/brand/svg-kit/`;
  - **production code:** `/home/user/elara/apps/mobile/src` (`TabBar.tsx`, `Icon.tsx`, `system/TornPaper.tsx`, `system/SectionHeading.tsx`, `circles/AnswerSlip.tsx`, `circles/tokens.ts`) and `packages/tokens` (`scale.ts`, `colors.ts`);
  - **real art:** `/home/user/elara/apps/mobile/assets/images/`.

**The short answer.** The palette is not the problem: cream, forest and ember are Elara's own, as set in the guidelines and `colors.ts`. The generic look comes from how they were used:
- everything is flat, with no paper;
- every block is the same bordered card;
- the icons, medallions and accent colours were drawn by an AI instead of taken from the app;
- the type has no scale contrast;
- the posts use the standard social-feed template.

The guidelines describe a product that is "made of paper: torn, layered, a little imperfect". The prototypes showed a cream UI kit.

## Checked and kept (not tells)
- **Forest primary buttons with cream text.** The owner asked for these, and they are production's `buttonForest`.
- **Pill buttons and chips.** The guidelines' shape language is "near-square cards and full pills". The contrast between the two is the point, so pills stay for things you press. What changes is that cards stop being the default container.
- **Fraunces 600 for titles; Instrument Sans for everything structural.** These are the production fonts. Production's `scale.ts` says "No italics anywhere", so no italic cut was added.
- **The torn edge under the band.** It is the brand's own paper language. It now gets a second, offset sheet.
- **Explorar's audio covers, Alejandra's photo, the film stills and the workshop plates.** These are already specific, real or composed from the app's paper (IMAGERY.md).

## Not fixed here, on purpose
- **Ember as the forward colour.** The guidelines say "ember for the one thing to do next". The owner asked for forest primary actions, so forest stays. Ember appears only as the tab's active glyph, the live dot and the focus ring, and terracotta text is used for section links and «1 nueva».
- **The guidelines' Fraunces italic voice** contradicts `scale.ts` ("No italics anywhere"). The code is newer, so Elara's lines use upright Fraunces (the `voice` role).

## What happened to each tell
The revision is recorded in `SHARED-SYSTEM.md`, under "Authored pass". After this audit, the owner narrowed the scope: a focused refinement, with no restyling of the type system. So the answers for T9 (one uppercase role: the paper label) and T11 (production's four-size scale and 24 pt margins) were **not applied**. They stay listed as remaining weaknesses. Every other tell was addressed.
