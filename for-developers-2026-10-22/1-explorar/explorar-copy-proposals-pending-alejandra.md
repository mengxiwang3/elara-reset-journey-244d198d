# Copy proposals (v2)

All proposed Spanish here is **BORRADOR · pendiente de Alejandra**. No em dashes are used, and no outcomes, statistics or quotes were invented.

**Viewing the proposals.** The prototype keeps the current catalogue text by default (verbatim DATA). The reviewer control «Texto: actual / propuesto» switches to the proposals. In proposed mode every changed string has a dotted ember underline, so nothing changes silently. Only one title change is proposed (row 7), and it appears only in proposed mode.

**Method.** Each line was checked against what the piece actually contains: its cues (the spoken lines), agenda, «¿Es para ti?» lines, or guide body, using the catalogue's own words. Vague reassurance («con suavidad», «una escucha corta», «una tarde para…») was replaced with the specific exercise, question or object in the piece.

The proposals follow that register.

## Flagged, not changed in the prototype

- **Spoken cue (needs re-recording).** «Dos minutos, antes de cualquier número.» is the first line of a 1:05 audio. It is part of the recording, so it cannot be fixed by text.
- **Alejandra's seed bio (not shown in the UI).** In `gen-explore-seed.mjs` it reads «Pongo la voz a la mayoría de las escuchas…». Every listen in the catalogue is a synthetic voice. If the bio is ever shown, it would wrongly say she voices them. It needs her own wording.
- **Time words.** Talleres are at 19:00 in New York (01:00 in Madrid), so «tarde» and «noche» in descriptions read wrongly in some zones. Rows 2 and 4 address the two on the overview path. «Dinero de noche» and «Una hora tranquila para los números que solo miras cuando oscurece» describe her habit rather than the clock, so they were left.
- **Durations, exhaustive.** I checked every audio and video against its measured length. The only contradictions are rows 6, 7–8, 16, 22 and 23; there are no others.
