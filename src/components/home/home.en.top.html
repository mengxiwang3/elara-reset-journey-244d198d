    For the woman who holds everything, even when no one notices.

    Elara helps you see where you are, what is asking for attention, and your next small step. Each destination is a short pause to look at a situation like these and leave with something you can use that same day.

    • Someone asks you for money and you do not yet know how much you can give.
    • You solve things for everyone and almost no one asks how you are.
    • You want to set a limit without a fight.
    • Fine on the outside; inside, unsure what you want next.

    Not English, translated. Spanish, understood. The weight of being the eldest daughter, the family that calls, the two worlds you live in. Also available in English.

    Built with them, not for them. More than 4,600 women answered our survey; almost 1 in 3 chose money as the first thing they would fix. That is why the practice above starts there.

    Practical questions

    What happens in a daily practice?

    You arrive somewhere, read a concrete situation, answer one question and leave with a tool and a souvenir. Like the practice above.

    How much time do I need?

    A few minutes a day. You go at your own pace and can pause whenever you like.

    Can I use it in Spanish or English?

    Both. Elara is built in Spanish first and is also in English.

    What happens to my answers, and how do I remove them?

    What you choose in this page’s demonstration is remembered only in your browser during the visit, and you can clear it with “Start again”. It is not app progress. In the app you can ask us to delete your account and data from this page. Details are in the Privacy Policy. Elara is not therapy or a medical service.
