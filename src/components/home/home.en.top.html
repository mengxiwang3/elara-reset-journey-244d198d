    Practical questions

    What happens in a daily practice?

    You read a situation, answer one question and leave with a tool and a souvenir, like above.

    How much time do I need?

    A few minutes a day, at your own pace.

    Can I use it in Spanish or English?

    Both. Elara is made in Spanish first and is also in English.

    What happens to my answers, and how do I remove them?

    Here, your choices are only remembered in your browser, and Start again clears them. In the app you can ask us to delete your account and data here; more in the Privacy Policy. Elara is not therapy or a medical service.
