Atlas: “Sorpresa” locked places

How the Atlas shows places a user has not reached yet. Only the next two places tease with a blurred photo and a “?”. Everything further ahead is a small coloured dot, and its name and photo stay hidden until the user gets there.

Chosen 2026-10-26: Sorpresa, “Maché natural” globe, “Postal garabato” postcards, big globe in every viewCopy: Spanish (EN to follow)Interactive prototype (final)The 4 directions, for contextPDF of this page

Screens

Chosen look

  • Globe: “Maché natural”. The sea is one blue with undyed cream paper strips over it. Land is tinted in the area colour (grey until the area is started). Seams, a soft rim shadow and a paper-grain overlay. No rainbow.
  • Size: 352 px across (radius 176) on a 390 px-wide screen, in both the world and zone views. In the zone view it only moves (centre at y 250 within the 500 px globe canvas) and rotates to the area. Keep it from feeling busy: other areas' places show only as dots while a zone is open.
  • Postcards: “Postal garabato”, 128 px wide. Torn paper edge, a hand-drawn frame in the area colour, a dotted stamp with the week number, name and country on one line each (they shorten with “…”). The row scrolls sideways and snaps.

The rule

Each place has a state from the user's progress, plus an order (o, 0-based) inside its area of life. Places inside an area open in order.

state(place)  ∈ done | current | open | locked          // from backend progress
lockRank(p)   = count of locked places in p.area with o < p.o
isTeaser(p)   = state(p) == locked
                && lockRank(p) < (areaStarted(p.area) ? 2 : 1)
isHidden(p)   = state(p) == locked && !isTeaser(p)
areaStarted(a)= any place in a is done or current

So a started area teases its next two places. An area that hasn't been started teases only its first place, so every area always shows at least one “?”.

Behaviour and edge cases

  • One tap opens. Tapping a postcard or a globe pin opens the place sheet straight away. The postcard row scrolls sideways, and a scroll never opens anything.
  • Dots aren't tappable. The area still opens by tapping its land, its label or its area card.
  • Pins near the rim. A pin facing less than about 0.32 toward the viewer becomes a dot. After pins are pushed apart so they don't overlap, they're clamped inside the globe edge so none hang off it. This applies to every state except the selected place.
  • No leaks. Hidden names and photos must not appear in alt text, accessibility labels, the list, “Leer más”, search, push notifications or share images. Ideally the API doesn't send them to the client at all until a place becomes a teaser.
  • Reveal moment. When a place goes from teaser to open, unblur the photo, drop the “?” and play a short pop (skip it if reduced motion is on). The place after it becomes the new teaser. This moment isn't built in the prototype.
  • Accessibility. The globe is a canvas, so the list is the screen-reader path. A teaser pin's label reads “Lugar sorpresa en {país}, siguiente”. Hidden places are grouped as “{n} lugares por descubrir” instead of one row each, if the list feels long to people using VoiceOver or TalkBack.
  • Small screens. At 320 px the zone title shortens with “…” and the postcard names are one line with “…”.

Data the client needs

Area   { id, name_es, region_es, color, tint, places: Place[] (ordered) }
Place  { id, areaId, order, state,                    // always
         country_es,                                  // teaser and up
         name_es, blurb_es, thumbUrl, heroUrl, lat, lon,  // teaser: thumb/hero only (to blur);
       }                                              // hidden: none of these
Pins for hidden places only need lat/lon, colour and order.

The prototype computes everything on the client from one static list. That's fine for a prototype, but not for production, because it ships every hidden name and photo to the phone.
