Open C v2

What changed from C

  • «Por qué hoy» opens the session, above the card preview. Its line no longer promises that the next days build on her choice, because day 2's content doesn't.
  • «Para llevarte» now asks: «¿Qué podrías averiguar sobre esta opción? Escribe una pregunta o un pequeño paso.»
  • Step 2: her choice in a compact row, then the field. Elara's note comes last and folds away while she types.
  • «Cambiar» keeps her draft. If she picks a different option, a note says so and Elara's note reloads.
  • Elara's notes now refer to what she actually wrote. They don't assume consequences, timing or a judgement of her answer, and stay marked «Respuesta simulada».
  • On Hoy, a compact takeaway card sits right under the greeting.
  • «Ninguna por ahora» is a real answer: step 2 becomes optional and she can «Terminar sin escribir». «Prefiero pasar» records nothing. Nothing is invented for a skipped question.
  • States shown: keyboard, unfinished draft, resume, changed answer, loading, offline (one «Reintentar», one «Seguir sin la nota de Elara»), each skip, completion. Saving and offline are tagged as simulated.
  • Text is 14px or larger, contrast is at least 4.5:1 (lowest 4.98:1), and every control is at least 44px. Layouts hold at 130% and 160% text.

C v2 no longer uses exactly the same copy and replies as A and B, so test it against C v1 or the current app, not in a three-way comparison. All of this is a hypothesis to test, and the new Spanish is pending María and Alejandra.

The three concepts

A

Step by step

One screen per moment: why today, step 1, Elara's note, step 2, Elara's note, a closing screen with tomorrow. Clearest, cheapest to build, slowest.

Open A
B

Conversation with Elara

One thread. Elara explains why today and asks; her answers become bubbles, and the choices and takeaway stay as structured inputs. Most likely to feel understood, most exposed to weak replies.

Open B
C

Action first, a card to keep

She sees an empty card first; each step fills it. The finished card stays on Hoy all week and in Tu colección. Clearest payoff and reason to return.

Open C

Each prototype has a reviewer panel to jump between screens and see the loading, offline, resumed and finished states.

Recommendation to test

C's card, with A's «Por qué hoy» line on the first screen. Showing what she leaves with before she answers makes the purpose concrete, and the card on Hoy gives a reason to come back that doesn't depend on how good Elara's reply is. It also costs less than B to build on the current app.

This is a hypothesis based on design reasoning and a few comments, not research. Keep B in the test: if testers feel much more understood with it, that outweighs its cost.

Suggested test: 5–6 testers, each doing this day in two of the three concepts (rotate the order), then asking «¿Para qué era hoy?», «¿Qué te llevas?», «¿Sentiste que Elara te escuchó?» and «¿Volverías mañana? ¿Por qué?».

  2. En la C, ¿te ayuda ver la tarjeta vacía al principio, o te parece una tarea más?

Suggested content changes

Proposals only. None is applied in the prototypes, so all three use the same current wording.

  • «Para llevarte»: Kara's «¿Qué podrías hacer esta semana para mejorar tu situación en tu trabajo actual?» ties it to her choice, but needs a version per option, or a template that quotes the option she chose.
  • A clearer version already exists in the app's built-in copy but not on the server: «Nombra una cosa sobre la parte que elegiste que podrías averiguar esta semana…». Check whether it was meant to ship.
  • One example under the box, for example «Por ejemplo: preguntar si hay formación gratuita».
  • «Ninguna por ahora» leads to the same takeaway, which then makes no sense. Skip or adapt it.
  • New prototype lines such as «Hoy eliges por dónde empezar a mirar tus ingresos…» need María and Alejandra's approval.

Keep in mind

  • These are HTML approximations of the app; spacing and bars are close, not exact.
  • Elara's replies are written by hand to the app's rules, not by the model. Judge the structure, not the wording.
  • In the app this is path week 2, day 1 (Wuzhen is the week-2 place), so the prototypes say «Semana 2 · Día 1».
  • Offline only simulates the reply failing. No screen-reader or large-text check yet.
