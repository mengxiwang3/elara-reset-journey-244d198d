Same family

Each option in ink over the colour mark. A and B use its exact sun; C moves the sun lower and right, onto the horizon and out to the orb's edge, and keeps the colour mark's horizon and lower circle.

Where C stands

C holds at 16 pt as one round shape, sun set into land, with a single horizon running across it. It does not form a ring or a pupil, and because the sun is wide and sits deep in the horizon there is no neck, so it does not read as a head and shoulders. The cost is that its sun is lower and larger than the colour mark's, so the family link is the orb's round shape and the horizon rather than an identical sun. Choose one of the four and I'll export it and wire it in.
