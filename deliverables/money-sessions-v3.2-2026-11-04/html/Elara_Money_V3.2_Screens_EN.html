How to read this document

  • Each day has four screens. What’s inside the frame is what she sees or hears.
  • Lines starting “If she taps…” appear only in that case.
  • “(supported answer)” is for review only: the app doesn’t mark it before she chooses.
  • Under each day: what to check and what changed from V3.1.

What changed from V3.1

  • No script gives away the answer before the question (days 1, 2, 4 and 5).
  • One rule per day: the same at the start, at the close and on day 7.
  • “Not sure, show me” on every question: she learns without having to guess.
  • Each day opens with yesterday’s rule in one line: a short recall that helps it stick.
  • “If you want to try it”: one optional idea to use outside Elara. Not a task; nobody asks about it later.
  • “I have a different question” opens the text field: one path, not two.
  • Day 3: her rehearsal phrase is the main action again. Day 7: two recall questions before she picks her rule.

What is still a hypothesis

Whether it truly helps can only be shown by testing with real women. Everything runs on replies written in advance; nothing is AI-generated.

  • Word choices: «alquiler» or «arriendo», and «la luz» or «los servicios» (day 1), since Colombia is the beta’s largest group; «respaldo», «colchón» or «guardadito» (day 2); «estado de cuenta» (day 4).

  • Whether the amounts feel realistic: rent US$180, electricity US$170, basket US$100.
  • Which financial regulator to name in each country (day 6).
  • Proposal: let the questions women write reach Ale, anonymously and only with their permission, for a weekly audio answer. Today they’re saved and nobody answers them.

The test

Test as text with 5-6 women before recording audio. The most telling question: the next day, before she opens the app, “Do you remember yesterday’s rule? Say it in your own words.” And at the end of the week: “Did you try any of the ideas outside the app?” No is a fine answer too.
