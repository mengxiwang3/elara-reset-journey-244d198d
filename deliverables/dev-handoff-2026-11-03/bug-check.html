03 · P2 · Transient keyboard overlap

Verdict: PLAUSIBLE (classify only). The code has two timing paths that produce “covered, then settles”.

Evidence. - hooks/useKeyboard.ts:38-46: iOS state updates on keyboardWillShow, which is fine. - useKeyboardAwareScroll (useKeyboard.ts:157-172) reveals the focused field twice. The first pass runs on keyboardWillShow, before the avoider has shrunk the scroller, and its scroll is clamped to 0 (the file says so at :161-164). This is used by the step screen (StepScreen.tsx:242, 88 pt gap) and the circle composer. - The daily step in the photo band: - BandDay.tsx:188-200 shrinks the band when keyboard.visible, with an Animated.timing of BAND_MS = 500 ms (photoDay.ts:70). That adds up to roughly 0.5-1 s of settling. - Pregunta ((tabs)/ask.tsx:178-184,361): KeyboardAvoidingView padding, plus scrollToEnd 50 ms after visible. Less likely to overlap.

Fix direction (not requested): - In BandDay, drive the band collapse with keyboard.duration and start it on keyboardWillShow. - Or move to react-native-keyboard-controller, which is a bigger change.
