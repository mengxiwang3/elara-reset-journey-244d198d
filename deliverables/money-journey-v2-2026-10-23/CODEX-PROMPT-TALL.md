# Prompt for Codex: tall vertical scenes (slide-up days)

Make each image as a single, continuous, ultra-tall portrait photograph.

**Size:** 1200 x 3200 px (3:8). If you cannot go that tall, 1080 x 2700 px is the minimum. Do not upscale a smaller image; render at this size natively. Export as PNG.

**Composition (important):**
- One continuous scene, one camera position. No collage, no split, no panels, no seams.
- Built to be read from top to bottom, like a phone scrolling up. Something worth looking at in every third of the image: top third (sky, distance, or the main landmark), middle third (the main subject or the street/water/terrain), bottom third (foreground detail: cobbles, flowers, rocks, water).
- Keep the main landmark in the middle third, not at the very top or bottom, so it survives cropping.
- Leave the sides lightly detailed. About 15% of each side edge may be cropped.

**Look:** realistic documentary photo, warm natural light, muted natural colour, gentle depth. No people, no vehicles, no signs, no readable text, no logos, no watermarks.
