# Codex prompt: scenes sized for the phone screen (weeks 2 to 5)

Make 28 scenic landscape images for a phone app background (Money Clarity, weeks 2 to 5, 7 days each). Use your image generation tool at the HIGHEST resolution it allows.

SIZE AND SHAPE (important)
- Wide panorama, 3:1 ratio, as large as possible. Target 4800 x 1600 px. Minimum 3600 x 1200 px. Never smaller than 1200 px tall. Do not upscale a small image; generate it large.
- It will be shown on a phone as a window about 390 x 665 points (about 1170 x 1995 px on a modern phone) that slowly slides across the picture. So fine detail must be sharp at 1200 px tall.
- Keep the main subject in the UPPER-MIDDLE of the frame (between 15% and 60% from the top). The bottom 40% is covered by an answer panel, so put only calm water, ground, grass or reflections there, no key details.
- Keep the left and right edges calm (no important subject cut by the edge) so it can be cropped.

LOOK (lighter, calmer)
- Bright, airy, high-key light: soft morning or late-morning light, gentle haze, pastel sky. Avoid night, deep blacks, heavy contrast, dark foregrounds, saturated neon.
- Realistic documentary photo, 35mm, natural colour, very clean and sharp, no blur, no painterly look.
- No people, no boats with people, no text, no signs, no logos, no watermarks, no crowds.
- One continuous scene, one camera position. No collage, no split, no panels, no vertical seam.

FILES
- Name them w{week}d{day}.png (for example w3d1.png), in a folder `panoramas/`.
- Tell me the exact pixel size of every file, and flag any that came out below 3600 x 1200.

PLACES (one place per week; give each day a different view of the same place so the week feels like a walk through it)
- Week 2: Wuzhen water town, China (canals, arched stone bridge, willows, calm water)
- Week 3: Yuanyang rice terraces, Yunnan, China (flooded terraces, soft mist)
- Week 4: Kyoto, Japan (Arashiyama bridge and river, Philosopher's Path, quiet rooftops)
- Week 5: Zhangjiajie, China (pillar mountains, Tianmen cave arch, soft cloud)

First make only w3d1 and w3d4 and show me. Wait for my OK, then do the rest.
