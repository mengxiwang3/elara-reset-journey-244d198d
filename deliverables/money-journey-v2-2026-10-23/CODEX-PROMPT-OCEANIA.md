# Prompt for Codex: Oceania scenes (Daily Rhythm, 7 days)

Make 7 photographs, plus 2 optional spares. They join a set that already exists (Asia, North America, Europe, South America, Africa), so they must match it in size and look.

This journey is about the shape of an ordinary day. **Each image is one time of day, in order: day 1 is dawn and day 7 is night.** The light should move through the week.

**Sizes (render natively, do not upscale):**
- **Vertical (V):** 1620 x 3888 px (5:12). Minimum 1080 x 2592.
- **Horizontal (H):** 3888 x 1620 px (12:5). Minimum 2592 x 1080.
- Export as PNG.

**Look:** realistic documentary travel photo, natural light true to the time of day, rich but natural colour, gentle depth, sharp detail throughout. No people, no vehicles, no boats with people, no signs, no readable text, no logos, no watermarks. Animals only where the table says so.

**Composition:**
- One continuous scene, one camera position. No collage, no panels, no seams.
- **Vertical images** scroll upward on a phone. Put something worth looking at in every third: sky or distance at the top, the main landmark in the middle third, and foreground detail at the bottom (rock, plants, water, sand).
- **Horizontal images** pan left to right. Keep the main landmark between 35% and 65% of the width, with interesting detail out to both edges.
- About 15% of each long edge may be cropped. Keep it lightly detailed.

## Check before sending
Check every image yourself. Regenerate once if you see:
- a seam
- a person
- readable text
- a distorted landmark (wrong number of Three Sisters, a misshapen Opera House or bridge)
- light that doesn't match its time of day
- anything that looks fake

If it still fails, keep the best version and mark it "needs review".
