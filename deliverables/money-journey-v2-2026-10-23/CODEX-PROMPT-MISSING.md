# Prompt for Codex: the missing scenes (Europe, South America, Oceania)

Make 6 photographs, plus 3 optional spares. They join a set that already exists, so they must match it in size and look.

**Sizes (render natively, do not upscale):**
- **Vertical (V):** 1620 x 3888 px (5:12). Minimum 1080 x 2592.
- **Horizontal (H):** 3888 x 1620 px (12:5). Minimum 2592 x 1080.
- Export as PNG.

**Look:** realistic documentary travel photo, warm natural light (golden hour or soft morning), rich but natural colour, gentle depth, sharp detail throughout. No people, no vehicles, no boats with people, no signs, no readable text, no logos, no watermarks.

**Composition:**
- One continuous scene, one camera position. No collage, no panels, no seams.
- **Vertical images** scroll upward on a phone. Put something worth looking at in every third: sky or distance at the top, the main landmark in the middle third, and foreground detail at the bottom (stone, flowers, water, sand).
- **Horizontal images** pan left to right. Keep the main landmark between 35% and 65% of the width, with interesting detail out to both edges.
- About 15% of each long edge may be cropped. Keep it lightly detailed.
