Fix plan

27 Oct 2026 · a solution for every issue in the UX walkthrough and every known gap in the handoff. 68 items: 18 before testers, 33 during the beta, 17 later. Effort: S under a day, M one to three days, L more. Spanish copy here is a proposal for María and Alejandra.

Suggested order. 1) A1 and A2, so nobody writes to production. 2) The «before testers» rows; most are small, and C1 and E2 are the largest. 3) G7, moving each area into the app as it passes review. 4) The other beta rows as testers report.
