# Curriculum audit: Purpose Finding (`purpose`, document prefix PURPOSE)

Read-only audit of branch `claude/curriculum-pass` in `/home/user/elara-w1`. Nothing in the app repo was changed.

Files read: `apps/mobile/src/content/pathDays/purpose.ts`, `apps/mobile/src/i18n/locales/{es,en}/pathCopy/purpose.ts`, the 35 `PURPOSE-*` rows of `tools/curriculum/curriculum-210.json`, the five `purpose` rows of `tools/curriculum/weeks-30.json`, `apps/mobile/src/content/pathCurriculum.test.ts`, `tools/curriculum/README.md`, `docs/DAY-STEPS.md`, `docs/GUIDE-VOICE.md`, `docs/SAFETY.md`, `teachingReply`/`authoredReply` in `packages/shared/src/practice.ts`, and Money day 3 in the money copy as the worked example.

How to read the status column:

- I compared every day's question, options, `ask` body and takeaway, in both languages, against the document rows by script. **All 35 days match the document word for word.** No Purpose id is in `REWRITTEN_TAKEAWAYS`, and nothing in the Purpose copy is marked BORRADOR/DRAFT. So every day's status is `document`.
- The feedback on every step is one of six rotating generic lines (three for `ask`, three for `take`), shared by all six paths (each `ask` line appears 12 times per path). Those are `placeholder`. A live model reply is tried first; these lines are the floor, and they are the only authored feedback in the area.
- Week intro notes, payoffs and week letters are `team-written` (the payoff wraps the document's week takeaway).
- No Purpose step uses `eyebrow`, `placeholder`, `prefill`, `replies` or `teach`.

Constraints that shape the fixes (from the test oracle and the engine):

- The test pins the `ask` title, kind, modes, options and the `take` body to the document. The `ask` body is not pinned. A rewritten `take` body needs its id added to `REWRITTEN_TAKEAWAYS`, may quote only `{ask}`, and needs a `bodyFallback`; it then becomes `rewritten-pending`.
- `teach` templates can quote only the same day (a template naming another day is ignored). Cross-day quotes are possible in `body` with `bodyFallback` (Money day 4 does this with `{d3.take}`) and, per `docs/DAY-STEPS.md`, in `prefill`/`prefillFallback`.
- Option-specific `replies` are honoured on any choice step (`authoredReply`), shown at once and stored.
- Template syntax below (`{ask}`, `{ask#1}`, `{d6.take}`) follows the docs and the Money example. I did not run any of the proposed templates.
