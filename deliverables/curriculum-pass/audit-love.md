## What holds for all 35 days (read once, not repeated in every row)

- **Status.** Every `ask` title, option list, ask body and `take` body is the document's wording (no LOVE id is in `REWRITTEN_TAKEAWAYS`; no BORRADOR/DRAFT marker in the love copy). So every day is labelled `document`. The other strings are not the document's:
  - `placeholder`: all 70 `elara` lines. Six generic sentences rotate by position, not by meaning ("Gracias. Con eso basta para trabajar.", "Eso ayuda. Puedes cambiarlo cuando quieras.", "Anotado. Nada de esto tiene que ser perfecto.", "Es tuyo para conservar, cambiar o dejar.", "Algo pequeño y claro es suficiente. Se queda contigo.", "Sea lo que sea que escribiste, puedes volver a ello."). The same six are used in Money.
  - `team-written`: the five week intro notes (one identical sentence, also identical to Money's), the payoff prefix ("Para llevarte esta semana:" + the document's week takeaway), the five letters and letter fallbacks, "Elige una." / "Elige las que encajen…", "Prefiero pasar".
- **Feedback now.** No Love day uses `eyebrow`, `teach`, `replies`, `prefill`, `placeholder` or a templated body. The only authored feedback is the rotating line above; anything specific depends on the live model. In the table, "Feedback now" gives the fallback line the step actually carries.
- **Shape.** Every day is `ask` + `take`, both optional. Every `take` is a blank write field titled "Para llevarte" whose body is an instruction, with no example and no starter.
- **What the test oracle pins.** Day title, ask title, ask kind/modes, options and (outside `REWRITTEN_TAKEAWAYS`) the take body must equal the document. Ask `body`, `eyebrow`, `teach`, `replies`, `placeholder`, `prefill` and intro notes are not pinned. So most fixes below need no oracle change; a take-body rewrite needs its id added to `REWRITTEN_TAKEAWAYS`; an option change needs the document changed first and breaks label-stored answers.
- **Scope check (partner, children, safe home).** No day names children or a home. "Pareja" appears once, as one option of six with "Prefiero no elegir" (D1). No day requires a partner. Two options assume the other person is a woman ("Tiempo juntas" D4, "Hacer algo juntas" D15). Several days assume there is a specific other person (D4, D5, D16, D19, D23 to D27); the document softens most with "hipotética", "inventado", "si alguna", but not D4, D5, D16, D19.
- **Safety check against `docs/SAFETY.md`.** The documented support route is: a path answer is screened server-side; a T2 match ("abuse toward her") keeps the answer, shows the fixed crisis card with the helpline link (`findahelpline.com`, provisional) and ends the day. The app's own support screen is "Pedir ayuda ahora" (`/crisis-help`, linked from Today and Ask per `helpNow.ts`). The curriculum respects this in that it never tells her to confront anyone and repeats "no hace falta enviarlo / iniciar esa conversación". Three gaps:
  1. Screening reads typed words. The two places where she can signal risk by **tapping** ("Temo una reacción" D9, "No veo esas condiciones" D26) match nothing in the lexicon and are answered by a generic line ("Anotado. Nada de esto tiene que ser perfecto." / "Eso ayuda. Puedes cambiarlo cuando quieras.").
  2. The copy says "prioriza apoyo" (D9), "Pedir apoyo" (D13), "elige buscar apoyo" (D25), "Buscar apoyo" (D27) and never says what support or where. Nothing in a Love day mentions "Pedir ayuda ahora".
  3. After "Temo una reacción" on D9, days 10 to 13 continue drafting a boundary for "esa situación" unchanged. `when` conditions only read the same day, so the path cannot branch across days.
- I did not check whether a day step can render a tappable link to `/crisis-help`; I found none in the step config. Fixes below name the screen in words.
