## What is true of every day (read this before the table)

- **G1. The only authored feedback is a placeholder.** All 70 steps carry one of six rotating lines (ES: «Gracias. Con eso basta para trabajar.», «Es tuyo para conservar, cambiar o dejar.», «Eso ayuda. Puedes cambiarlo cuando quieras.», «Algo pequeño y claro es suficiente. Se queda contigo.», «Anotado. Nada de esto tiene que ser perfecto.», «Sea lo que sea que escribiste, puedes volver a ello.»). They are assigned by position, not by meaning: 11 or 12 uses each. The README says a model reply comes first and these lines are the floor, so what she actually sees depends on the model; with the model off, on a timeout, or on the photo day (no reply beat), nothing explains the concept or refers to her answer. In the table "Feedback now" is therefore "placeholder (G1)" everywhere, and no day is rated OK.
- **G2. Every takeaway is a blank write field under a one-line instruction.** No takeaway has a placeholder, example or prefill. Several instructions are not writing tasks at all (they ask her to do something off-screen, or to "keep" what she just wrote), so it is unclear what she should type.
- **G3. Nothing is ever shown back.** Questions say «ese momento», «ese cambio», «ese primer paso», «lo probaste», «tu plan» without quoting what she wrote earlier. If she skipped the earlier day (every step is optional) the question has no referent.
- **G4. Team-written frame.** Each week's intro note is the same sentence five times («Cada pregunta es opcional, y nada aquí te pide contactar a nadie, gastar dinero ni compartir.»): it says nothing about the week. The payoff is the document's week takeaway behind a team-written prefix. The week letters and their fallbacks are team-written and generic apart from one quote.
- **Constraint on fixes.** Options and question titles are pinned to the document by the test; takeaway bodies are pinned unless the row is listed in `pathRevisions/everyday.ts`. So the fixes below prefer fields the test does not pin: `eyebrow`, ask `body` (+ `bodyFallback`), `placeholder`, `placeholders` (blanks), `prefill` (+ `prefillFallback`), `replies`, `teach`. Where a fix changes a takeaway body or a question it says so, and that row would become `rewritten-pending`.
- **Technical facts used.** `teach` templates may quote only the same day (`{ask}`, `{ask#1}`, `{take}`); a template that names another day is ignored. Cross-day quotes (`{d5.ask}`) work in `body` and `prefill`, and fall back whole to `bodyFallback`/`prefillFallback`. A `replies` line for the chosen option wins over `teach`.
