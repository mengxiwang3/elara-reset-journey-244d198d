Elara Circles · life areas and life stages · design prototype, not in the app

Join the Circles that fit your life. Read first, ask when you're ready.

Women join several Circles, based on what they care about or are going through. A Circle is an open topic community; a room is an optional, private group. Click through the phone, or jump to a screen. Three paths are marked: a new woman choosing Circles, a returning woman reading and replying, and a woman joining another Circle later.
