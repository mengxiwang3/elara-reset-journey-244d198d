When a member can't answer this week

Two options, kept apart. The baseline rule is unchanged.

The baseline is: reading opens after writing. A woman who can't write this week has to be treated kindly without quietly redefining that rule. These are alternatives for you to decide between, not a change I've made.

Option A · passing does not open the room

“Not this week”

A private choice. Nothing is posted, nothing is visible to others, and she isn't reminded again this week. This week's answers stay closed to her unless she writes before Sunday. Her seat and the shelf are unchanged.

Option B · passing opens the room

“Pass, with a word”

She writes a short note, or picks one, and it takes the place of an answer. The note is visible to the room beside the answers, and her seat marks as passed. It opens this week's answers for her.

Key states at 390 × 844 and 320 × 568

A 84-word answer, keyboard up, and larger text (130 %)

When the keyboard is up, the question collapses to two lines, the audience label stays pinned in the header, and the send button stays above the keyboard. With larger text, long content scrolls and a fade marks where it continues; nothing in the header or the button row moves.

Larger text here is a 130 % scale in the prototype, not a real iOS Dynamic Type test. I haven't tried these on a device, and I haven't tested at the largest accessibility sizes.

Verify before real-member testing

The design says these things. This prototype proves none of them.

1 · Server-side audience enforcement

  • The design promises “Private · 8 women”, a cap of ten, and “their words open after yours”. All three have to hold on the server, not only in the screens. The room should return no answer text at all to a member who hasn't written this week, not hidden text.
  • Check every path, not just the room screen: shelf, recap, replies, push and email text, realtime, digests, search, direct messages and report flows.
  • Join and leave: a hard cap of ten under simultaneous joins, and a member who leaves or is removed loses access at once.
  • From a read of the repo, not a full audit: docs/CIRCLES-BETA-HANDOFF.md has room capacity adjustable by admins up to 20, and a keyword search of the API, mobile source and docs found no write-before-read gate. Treat both as rules that don't yet exist until shown otherwise.

2 · Recap consent

  • The design shows per-answer opt-in, default off. Confirm that answers without opt-in are never sent to the model, never quoted, and never counted in the recap's summary.
  • Decide a minimum number of opted-in answers before a recap is generated, so a pattern can't point to one person.
  • Check withdrawal: turning recap off later should remove it from future recaps and from stored recap inputs.
  • Check the consent line (“built from 5 answers”) is generated from real data, not typed copy.

3 · Decisions I made that you may want to change

  • A skipped week on the shelf reads “open after you write a late line”.
  • Replies are visible to the whole room, not only the author. The composer says so.
  • “This resonated” is private to the author, shown as names, never a count. The earlier approved wording was “This stayed with me”.
  • Elara is never in a seat or counted among the women.
